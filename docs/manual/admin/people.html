Manual / Administration

People and accounts

Adding colleagues, setting who approves for whom, and deactivating leavers.

Users is the roll of everybody with an account. It is where a new colleague gets one, where somebody's roles change, where you set who approves their requests, and where a leaver is switched off. Nothing on this screen deletes a person: accounts are deactivated, never removed, so the requests and payments they touched keep their name on them.

Reading the list

ColumnWhat it holds
NameThe person, as the product will show them everywhere else.
EmailWhat they sign in with. Stored lower-case, and it must contain an @.
RolesEvery role they hold, as a pill each. A person can hold several at once.
Default approverWho their own requests go to. An em dash means none is set.
StatusActive or Inactive. Only active people can be chosen as an approver.
EditOpens the panel for that person.

The sub-line under the heading — "A person can hold several roles at once" — is the thing to hold on to. Roles add up. Somebody who holds both Requester and Manager can raise requests and decide other people's, and their menu is the union of the two.

Adding somebody

  1. Select ＋ Add user at the top right.
  2. Type their Email. This is what they will sign in with.
  3. Type their Name.
  4. Choose a Role.

    This field offers two values only — Data entry and Admin. It is the account-level flag, not the role list; the fuller set of roles is assigned afterwards from the edit panel.

  5. Type a starting Password.

    At least 12 characters, and it must contain a letter and a number. Anything shorter is refused.

  6. Leave Active ticked unless you are creating an account that should not be usable yet.
  7. Select Add User.

    The new account appears in the list, and the creation is written to the audit trail.

What the Role field actually does

The Role choice on this panel also seeds the person’s first role: choosing Admin gives the new account the Admin role, and choosing Data entry gives it Accounts. If neither is what you want, open the new row’s Edit panel straight away and set the roles properly.

The starting password

There is no self-service password reset anywhere in the product, so the password you type here is the one the person will use to sign in for the first time. Give it to them over a channel you trust, and ask them to tell you if they cannot get in.

Changing somebody

FieldWhat it is for
NameRequiredHow they appear on every request, approval and payment.
RolesOptionalOne tick box per role, each with its description: Accounts — Process approved requests and record payments., Admin — Full administrative access., Manager — Review and decide on payment requests., Requester — Raise and manage your own payment requests. Tick as many as apply.
Default approver for their own requestsOptionalWho their requests go to by default. The person themselves is never in their own list — the hint under the field says so, and the save refuses it again on the server. None is a valid answer for somebody who raises nothing.
Reset passwordOptionalLeave blank to keep the current one. Anything you type replaces it immediately, with the same 12-character, letter-and-number rule as a new account.
ActiveOptionalUntick to switch the account off. See below.
  1. Select Edit on the person’s row.
  2. Change what needs changing.
  3. Select Save user.

    The name, the roles and the default approver are saved together in one go, so a save that is refused leaves none of them applied.

Opening the panel and saving from it

The Edit control on this screen carries no permission check of its own, so anybody who can open the people screen can open the panel. Saving is a separate matter: the save is refused unless you hold the create or edit grant for users. Opening the panel is not the same as being allowed to save from it. In practice only administrators can view this screen at all.

Deactivating a leaver

  1. Select Edit on their row.
  2. Untick Active.
  3. Select Save user.

Their status pill changes to Inactive and they can no longer sign in. Everything they raised, approved or paid stays exactly where it is, still carrying their name.

Check what was pointing at them

Deactivating somebody does not move the requests waiting on them. If they were an approver, reassign anything sitting in their queue first, and change the default approver on every person who pointed at them — an inactive user is refused as an approver with the chosen approver is not an active user, but only when that record is next saved.

The last administrator

The product will not let the last active administrator be switched off, and it will not let you switch off or demote your own administrator account. The refusals read at least one active administrator is required and You cannot deactivate or demote your own administrator account. Create the replacement administrator before you retire the old one.

Setting a default approver for everybody is worth the half hour it takes. It is what makes a request route itself, and the Configuration screen can be set so that the default is the only choice a requester gets.

Somebody has forgotten their password.

Open their Edit panel, type a new one in Reset password, and select Save user. It takes effect immediately. There is no reset email.

Why can I not pick a person as their own approver?

Self-approval is blocked throughout the product. The select leaves them out of their own list, and the save refuses it a second time with a user cannot be their own default approver.

I want to remove an account entirely.

You cannot, and that is deliberate. Deactivate it instead — the history has to keep the name attached to it.

Somebody holds two roles and I only meant to give them one.

Open Edit, untick the role you did not mean to give, and save. The change applies on their next request; there is nothing to restart.

Where do I see what a role actually grants?

Roles and permissions. Select the role's tab and read the matrix.
