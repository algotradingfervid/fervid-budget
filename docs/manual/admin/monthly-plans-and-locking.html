Monthly plans and locking

Opening a month, closing it, and what a locked month refuses.

Budgets live per month, so every month has to be opened before anything can be budgeted in it. A month is opened here, reviewed here, and — once the figures are settled and reported — locked, which stops anybody writing into it by accident.

The monthly plans screen

ColumnWhat it shows
MonthThe month, with the number of heads it covers underneath.
StatusOpen or Locked. A locked month also shows the date and time it was locked.
SourceThe month its budgets were copied from, or Manual if it was started blank.
BudgetThe total budgeted across every head in the month.
ActualThe total actually paid in the month.
RemainingBudget minus actual.
UsedActual as a percentage of budget.
CreatedWhen the plan was opened.
ActionsGrid, Budget, Payments and Report — the same month, on four different screens.

Opening a month

  1. Pick the month in New month.
  2. Choose a Plan type.

    Copy from month brings last month’s budget figures over as a starting point. Start blank opens the month with no figures at all.

  3. If you are copying, pick the Source month.

    The source has to have budgets in it. Copying from a month with none is refused with source month has no budgets to copy.

  4. Select Create Month.

    The month appears at the top of the table as Open, and you can go straight to Budget to adjust the figures.

Copying is almost always right. Most heads carry the same figure month after month, and adjusting a dozen of them on Budgets is quicker than typing 229 from scratch.

A month that is already locked cannot be recreated — the create is refused with the locked-month message. Unlock it first if you genuinely need to rebuild its plan.

Locking a month

The lock control is not on the monthly plans screen. It lives in a Month Close panel at the foot of the variance grid for that month, and it is drawn only for somebody whose role carries the month lock permission — the Process column of the Budgets & variance grid row on Roles and permissions.

  1. Open Variance grid and pick the month you want to close.
  2. Scroll to the Month Close panel at the foot of the screen.

    The line above the form counts what you might want to look at first: how many budgeted heads are unpaid, how many have unbudgeted spend, and how many are over budget across the entire month. Zero-budget, zero-activity heads are separately identified and excluded from unpaid counts.

  3. Type a Lock reason.

    It is required. The reason goes into the audit trail and is shown on the grid while the month stays locked.

  4. Select Lock Month and confirm.

    The browser asks "Lock 2026-03? Budgets and payments will become read-only." first.

The month’s row on the monthly plans screen changes to a Locked pill with the time it was locked underneath.

What a locked month refuses

AttemptWhat happens
Saving budgets for that monthRefused. The screen disables every box and says so before you try.
Recording a payment dated in that monthRefused, and the payment form warns before you fill it in.
Editing a payment dated in that monthRefused.
Voiding a payment dated in that monthRefused.
Opening a month plan for that monthRefused.
Approving a request whose required-by date falls in that monthRefused, because an approval is a promise the request can be paid and it could not be.

The general refusal reads "This month is locked. Unlock it with a reason before changing budgets or payments." The approval refusal is more specific: it names the month, and suggests either reopening it or changing the required-by date.

A lock stops writes, not reads. Every screen still shows the month, the grid still totals it, and the reports still cover it. The variance grid marks the month with a Locked pill and a bar naming who locked it and why.

Unlocking a month

  1. Open the variance grid for the locked month.
  2. Scroll to the Month Close panel, which now offers Unlock Month.
  3. Type an Unlock reason.

    It is required, exactly as the lock reason is. An empty reason is refused — the product will not let a closed period be reopened anonymously.

  4. Select Unlock Month and confirm the "Unlock 2026-03 and allow changes again?" prompt.

Unlocking is not selective

Reopening a closed month reopens it for everything at once — budgets, new payments, payment edits and voids. If you only needed to correct one figure, lock the month again as soon as the correction is in.

The reason is the record

Both halves are recorded. The audit trail carries a Month Lock entry for each, reading "Locked 2026-03: …" and "Unlocked 2026-03: …" with your reason after the colon, and a Before / after expander beside it.

Where is the Lock button on the monthly plans screen?

There is not one. The Actions column offers Grid, Budget, Payments and Report only; locking is done from the Month Close panel at the foot of the variance grid.

I cannot see the Month Close panel at all.

It is drawn only for a role that holds the month lock permission. Check the Process column on the Budgets & variance grid row of Roles and permissions.

An accountant says they cannot record a payment for last month.

Check the month’s status on this screen. If it reads Locked, either the payment belongs in an open month or the month has to be reopened with a reason.

Does locking a month stop the reports?

No. Reading is untouched. A lock only refuses writes.

Can I lock a month that was never opened?

Yes — locking opens the month plan first if it is missing, then locks it. In practice you will have opened it long before, to budget in it.
