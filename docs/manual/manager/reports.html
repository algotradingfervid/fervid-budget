Manual / Approving

Reports

The monthly, by-project and by-head views, and what each answers.

Reports in the sidebar is the same budget-against-actual comparison as the variance grid, rolled up three different ways and over any range of months you choose. Where the grid answers how is this month going, reports answer how has this been going.

The line under the heading always names the range and the view you are looking at — 2026-07 to 2026-07 · monthly view.

The three views

Monthly, Projects and Heads are three tabs over one set of figures. Each takes the same date range and totals it at a different level, so the same money appears in all three and only the grouping changes.

TabOne row isThe question it answers
MonthlyOne month, for the whole organisationAre we spending against plan overall, and is it getting better or worse?
ProjectsOne project, in one monthWhich projects are running hot?
HeadsOne expense head within one project, in one monthExactly which budget line is the problem?

Setting the range

From and To are month pickers, not dates — the smallest range is a single month. Select Run to apply them. Current month resets both to the month you are in.

Ask for several months and the monthly view gives you one row each; the projects and heads views repeat their rows for every month in the range, with the month in the Period column.

Reading the table

ColumnWhat it holds
PeriodThe month, written 2026-07.
ProjectOn the projects and heads views only.
HeadOn the heads view only.
BudgetWhat was budgeted for that row in that month.
ActualWhat was paid.
RemainingBudget minus actual. Green while there is money left, red once it is negative.
UsedActual as a percentage of budget.

The four cards above the table — Budget, Actual, Remaining, Used — total the whole range, whichever view you are on. They are the same figures the variance grid puts at the top of its month.

A row with an empty range says No report rows for this range. That means no budget was set and no payment was made in those months, not that the report failed.

What the figures count

These are payments, not approvals

Actual is money that has been paid. Nothing you approve appears in any of these three views until the accounts team settles it, and voided payments and recoverable spend are left out entirely. Reports are a record of what happened, not a forecast of what is committed.

Because the figures are built the same way as the grid's, a number here will always agree with the same number there for the same month. If they disagree, one of them is being read for a different month.

Exporting

Export CSV sits at the top right of every view. The download is gated on a separate export permission which the approver role does not carry, so selecting it returns the Forbidden screen rather than a file.

If you need the figures as a spreadsheet, ask somebody in the accounts team or an administrator — both roles carry the export permission. The one export an approver can use is Export CSV on the approvals queue, which downloads the requests routed to you.

Run Heads across the last three or four months before approving a large request. A head that has been over budget every month is a different conversation from one that has drifted once.

The totals at the top do not match the rows I can see.

They total the whole range, not the visible page. On the heads view a wide range produces a great many rows.

A project appears with a budget and no spend at all.

Nothing was paid against it in that month. Approved but unpaid requests do not appear in any report.

Can I report on one project only?

Not from this screen — the only filter is the date range. Use the Search box on the variance grid to narrow to one project or head within a month.

Export CSV gave me a Forbidden page.

That is the permission, not a fault. See What an approver cannot see.
