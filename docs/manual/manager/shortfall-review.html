Reviewing a shortfall

Decide whether a recorded unpaid balance stays payable or is permanently closed.

Accounts can ask you to review a payment shortfall. Open the notification or the request's Decide the partial payment action. Review the approval, cumulative paid amount, remaining balance, recorded payment and Accounts' explanation.

The decisions

ActionUse it whenWhat happens
Keep balance payableThe unpaid amount will be paid later.Add the required plan note. The request returns to Approved without a reservation. Accounts claims it again and records another payment under the same approval.
Accept and closeYou accept permanently closing the unpaid balance.The confirmation names the write-off. The request becomes Completed — partial accepted and cannot be paid again.
Raise a concernYou need Accounts to explain the short payment.A required comment records the concern and tells Accounts. Status stays under review.

Accepting is a permanent closure

Do not accept and close a balance that is still owed next week. Use Keep balance payable and record the plan. Closing does not reverse a payment or move money, and reopening a completed request is not supported.

Discussing the decision

The conversation retains questions and replies with the financial history. Raising a concern leaves the payment unchanged. Only the assigned approver with the relevant permission can decide the shortfall; other readers can follow the history their access allows.

Installments without a shortfall decision

When Accounts chooses Installment — pay the balance later, the request returns directly to Approved for the remaining balance. The prior approval continues to apply. When total payments clear the balance, full settlement closes the request.
