Manual / Approving

What an approver can do

The five decisions available to you, and the limits of the role.

An approver decides whether money is allowed to leave. You do not raise the requests and you do not pay them — somebody asks, you decide, and the accounts team settles what you approved.

The product routes each request to one person by name. That person is the request's approver, and while the request waits, it waits on them. Your home screen opens on what is waiting on you.

The decisions you take

Every decision is taken on one request at a time, from the request's own screen. There is no bulk approval — the approvals queue says so at the foot of the page.

Every one of these is a two-press action. The control in the bar along the bottom opens a panel; nothing is written until you press the button inside that panel. Closing the panel changes nothing.

Whose decision it is

The action panels appear only for the person the request was routed to. Holding the approver role is not enough: if you open somebody else's request you see the whole thing — the amounts, the purpose, the conversation — with no action bar at the bottom.

Reassignment is the one exception, and it is deliberate. It is offered to anybody who holds the reassign permission, because it is the way out when the routed approver has left or can no longer act. Reassigning an approval explains it.

What you can see

Your request scope is the whole organisation, so My requests in the sidebar opens every request in the product, not only the ones addressed to you — the heading on that screen reads All requests to say so. The approvals queue is the narrower view: only the rows where you are the approver.

Two reporting screens come with the role. The variance grid shows budget against actual for every head in a month, and Reports roll the same figures up by month, by project or by head. Both are worth a look before you approve something large.

What the role does not carry

You cannot raise a request, record a payment, open the payments ledger, see the vendor master or the recoverables register, or download the documents attached to a request. None of that is a fault or a setting on your own account — the approver role does not hold those permissions. What an approver cannot see sets out each one and what to do instead.

If a request is fixable, return it. Returning keeps the number, the history and the thread, and lets the requester correct it. Rejection cannot be undone.

I can see a request but there are no buttons at the bottom.

Then you are not its approver. Look at the Approver field in the Details block — that names the person who has to decide. If they cannot, reassign it.

Can I approve a request I raised myself?

No. The product refuses a self-approval outright, and the approver list a requester chooses from never contains their own name.

Where do I find a request that has already been paid?

In My requests, which reaches every request. The approvals queue stops at approved, rejected and cancelled — see The approvals queue.
