Manual / Raising requests

Tracking your requests

The list, its tabs and filters, and how to tell at a glance what is waiting on you.

My requests is the one screen you will live in. It holds every request you have ever raised, divided into four tabs, and every card on it ends with a plain sentence naming whoever owes the next move.

The screen

The heading is followed by N shown · sorted by who is holding them up. That sort is literal: urgent requests come first, and after them the oldest, because the request nobody has touched for a fortnight is the one that needs finding. At the top right sit ⤓ Export CSV and + New request.

The four tabs

Each tab is its own address, so a view can be bookmarked or pasted to somebody. The number beside each label is the count for that tab under the filters currently applied.

TabWhat is in it
OpenEverything still live: awaiting approval, returned for correction, approved and awaiting payment, cancellation requested, With Accounts, and Partial — manager review. An installment with a remaining balance is open.
Needs meOnly what cannot move until you act — for a requester, the requests your approver returned.
ClosedAll terminal outcomes: Completed, Completed — partial accepted, Rejected, Withdrawn and Cancelled. Closed includes requests settled through payment as well as those ended without payment.
AllEvery request you have raised, whatever its state.

Under the same search, Type, Treatment and access scope, Open plus Closed equals All. Needs me is an overlapping action view, not a third partition. Switching tabs retains the active filters, so the counts and displayed cards describe the same scope.

Needs me

This is the tab worth checking first. For somebody who only raises requests it collects the ones your approver sent back for correction, and nothing else — an approved request waiting on Accounts is not yours to move, so it does not appear here.

Closed

Find completed full settlements and accepted shortfalls here alongside rejected, withdrawn and cancelled requests. The status badge identifies how each request ended; closure does not mean it was paid in full.

All

Use All to see both outstanding work and every terminal outcome without changing the other filters.

Reading a card

Each card carries, in order: the request number at the left and the amount at the right; the short title you gave it; a meta line naming the type and, where there is one, the project, the head and the invoice number; and a foot with the status pill on the left and the waiting sentence on the right. A recoverable request also shows a violet Recoverable pill naming its category, and an urgent one a red Urgent pill.

The full set is in The request lifecycle.

A hold is not a status. When Accounts park an approved request, its card still reads Approved — awaiting payment, but the sentence beside it changes to Waiting on you. The On hold pill and the question they asked appear when you open the request. See Getting paid.

Searching and filtering

Above the tabs is a toolbar with three controls and an Apply button. Search matches the request number, the payee, the invoice number, the purpose, the short title and the requester's name — its placeholder lists most of them: Number, payee, invoice, purpose…. Type narrows to one of the four request types. Treatment narrows to Budget expense or Recoverable.

When nothing matches, the body reads No requests match. Try another tab, or clear the filters. Note that the tab counts fall to zero with it — the counts are computed under the same filters as the rows, so a tab can never promise a number the list does not show.

Filters survive when you change tabs, and they are in the address. If a colleague asks what happened to a particular invoice, search for the invoice number on All and send them the resulting address.

When the list is longer than the page

A very long list is drawn in pages. The sub-line then reads N of M shown, and a row appears beneath the cards saying Showing 1–200 of 214 with ← Newer and Older → buttons. Both carry every filter with them, so paging never quietly widens the set.

Exporting

⤓ Export CSV downloads the list as a spreadsheet file with one row per request and these columns: Number, Status, Type, Title, Amount, Payee, Requester, Approver, Created. The export carries every matching row — it is never truncated the way the screen is.

The export follows the tab you are on and the text in Search, but not the Type and Treatment dropdowns. If those matter to you, filter the spreadsheet afterwards.

A request I raised is missing from every tab.

Clear the search box and the two dropdowns, then look at All. The counts drop with the filters, so an active filter can empty a tab completely.

I cannot see a colleague’s request.

You are not meant to. A requester sees their own requests and nothing else, and an address pointing at somebody else’s answers Not Found.

Why is an old request above a new one?

Because the list is sorted by who has been kept waiting longest — oldest first, with urgent requests ahead of everything. It is a work queue, not a diary.

What is the difference between Withdrawn and Cancelled?

You withdrew it yourself before anybody decided; a cancellation happens after approval and is your approver’s decision. See Asking to cancel an approved request.
