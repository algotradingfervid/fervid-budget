Manual / Paying

Paying part of a request

Record the payment and make an explicit decision about the unpaid balance.

Enter the amount that actually left the bank. The confirmation compares the approved amount, payments already recorded, this payment, and the remaining balance. A short payment has no settlement choice selected for you.

Choose what happens to the balance

ChoiceWhen to use itResult
Installment — pay the balance laterPart has been paid and the rest remains payable under this approval.The payment is recorded, the reservation is released, and the request returns to Approved. Accounts can claim it again for another installment.
Fully settledNo further amount is owed. For a short payment, explain the agreed deduction or adjustment.The request closes. The explanation is required when the paid total is short of the approval; the product does not calculate tax.
Ask manager to accept a shortfallYou need the approver to decide whether to close an unpaid balance.A reason is required. The request moves to Partial — manager review.

Record an installment

  1. Claim the approved request from the Accounts queue.
  2. Record the actual amount, date, payment mode and reference, and any proof.
  3. Open the confirmation and choose Installment — pay the balance later.
  4. Check the remaining balance and confirm.
  5. For the next transfer, claim the same request again. The next payment cannot exceed the remaining approved balance.

Each saved payment is permanent. Multiple installments share the original request and approval; they are not edits to earlier payments. Confirming the same submission twice does not create a second transfer record. Cancellation is unavailable after any payment has been recorded.

When the approver reviews a shortfall

The approver can Keep balance payable with a required plan note, which returns the request to Approved for further payment; Accept and close, which permanently writes off the unpaid balance from this request; or Raise a concern, which records the question and leaves the request under review.

Do not use Accept and close merely to arrange an installment. See Reviewing a shortfall.

Following the balance

The request shows cumulative payments and what remains. Installments awaiting payment return to the Accounts queue. Closed full settlements and accepted shortfalls remain closed; correcting a mistaken closure needs an agreed accounting process, not rewriting a saved payment.
