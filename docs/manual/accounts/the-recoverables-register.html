Manual / Paying

The recoverables register

Track money paid out, record returns or reconciliations, and retain the history.

Recoverables are money expected back, such as employee advances and deposits. They do not count as budget actuals. The payout request's approval/payment status is separate from whether the money has been recovered.

Read the balances

Outstanding is active recoverable payouts less recorded returns and reconciliations. Unpaid requests do not add to outstanding or overdue money. Past expected return and Due in 30 days apply to money still outstanding; paid-out-this-month describes outgoing recoverable payments.

Rollups group this balance by category and counterparty. Employee advances identify the employee even when no separate counterparty was entered. The register obeys your request data scope.

Filter the register

Use Search, Category and Ageing on desktop or open Filters on mobile, choose the options, then Apply filters. Reset clears them. The Reconciled ageing option includes items whose paid balance has been cleared; their history is retained.

One recoverable

The detail shows Total paid out, Returned or reconciled and Outstanding balance, followed by the payment and Recovery history. Conversation notes remain useful for chasing a counterparty, but they do not change the financial balance.

Record a return or reconciliation

  1. Open the recoverable and select the recovery form. You need recoverable report access, payment creation permission and access to that request.
  2. Choose Recovery type: Money returned, Expense reconciled or Balance adjustment.
  3. Enter Amount received or reconciled (₹) and Recovery date.
  4. Provide the Bank / accounting reference and Evidence and explanation. All fields are required; retain the underlying bank advice or accounting evidence outside this form and describe it.
  5. Check and confirm the permanent entry.

The amount must be positive and within both the current and historical balance at that date. A recovery cannot precede the first payout or be future-dated. Each entry reduces outstanding; the last one marks the balance Reconciled while retaining the history.

Accounting and corrections

Expense reconciliation and balance adjustment record a referenced accounting decision; they do not automatically post an expense to the budget. Recovery entries are immutable and have no edit/delete reversal flow. Check the evidence before confirming and use your organisation's accounting correction process for mistakes.

Recovery does not change the payout request's status, and saving an outgoing payment does not mean its recoverable has been returned.
