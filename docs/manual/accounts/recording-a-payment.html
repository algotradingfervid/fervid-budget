Manual / Paying

Recording a payment

Record real money movement against an approved request, then confirm its effect on the balance.

Every new payment belongs to an approved request. Open Accounts queue and take a request for processing, or choose one from the payment picker. Only the reservation holder may record it.

Enter the transfer

The request card supplies the payee, project/head, invoice and approved amount. Check previously paid and remaining figures before entering another installment.

  1. Enter Amount actually paid. It must be positive and no greater than the approval minus all existing active payments.
  2. Enter the date the money left, an available payment mode and the transaction/reference number. Future dates and locked months are refused.
  3. Attach any advice or proof and add the processing note. Saved request-linked payments and their documents are permanent.
  4. Open the settlement confirmation. This preview writes no payment.

Confirm the balance outcome

If the payment clears the remaining balance, confirm it as Fully settled. If it is short, explicitly select Installment — pay the balance later, Fully settled with a required explanation of the deduction/agreed adjustment, or Ask manager to accept a shortfall with a required reason.

Paying part of a request explains all three paths. An installment preserves the approval for the remaining amount; a full settlement closes it; a requested shortfall decision waits for the manager.

After confirming

The saved payment screen shows the immutable payment, proof and history. The requester and approver can follow the request outcome. Another installment requires a fresh reservation on the same request. The payment ceiling is recalculated when confirming, so an old form cannot spend past the remaining approval.

Repeated confirmation of the same submission leads to the already recorded payment. It does not create another installment. Saved payments cannot be edited or cancelled; see Correcting a payment.
