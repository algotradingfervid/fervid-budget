Manual / Paying

What the accounts role can do

The settlement job end to end, and what is deliberately out of your hands.

The accounts role turns approved requests into money that has left the bank. Everything you do starts from a request somebody else raised and somebody else approved: there is no way to create a payment out of nothing, and no screen that offers one.

The shape of the day is the same every day. Find the request, claim it so nobody else pays it too, record what actually left the bank, and say whether that settles the obligation. The product holds you to that order.

The job, end to end

  1. Open the payment queue and find the request.

    The queue is also the sidebar item Accounts queue, and its badge is the number of approved requests nobody has claimed.

  2. Select Take for processing, which claims the request for you.

    From that moment nobody else can pay it. It shows as processing and the queue names you as the holder.

  3. Record the payment — the amount, the date, the mode and the transaction reference.
  4. Confirm on the second step whether this is Fully settled or a Partial payment.

    A full settlement closes the request. A partial one hands the difference to the approver — see paying part of a request.

  5. The payment lands in the payments ledger, where it can be read but not changed.

What is deliberately not yours

Several things you might expect to find are missing on purpose. Each one belongs to somebody else, and the product will not let the accounts role reach around them.

Not yoursWhose it is
Approving, returning or rejecting a requestThe approver it was routed to. You never see an approve control at all.
Raising a requestThe requester. The accounts role holds no create permission, so My requests lists what you can see rather than what you can start.
Deciding a shortfall you recordedThe request’s own approver — see reviewing a shortfall.
Taking a claim off a colleagueAn administrator. You can release a claim you hold; you cannot take one you do not.
Changing a payment once it is savedNobody. A payment tied to a request is sealed — see correcting or voiding a payment.
Recording money coming back on a recoverableOut of scope in this version, and the register says so on screen.

Two accounts colleagues can still see different rows. What a role may do is one thing; which records it may see is a separate setting per role. The permission matrix lists both as the running system has them.

The one irreversible step

Confirming a payment saves it, and nothing afterwards can edit or cancel it. Check the amount, the date and the reference on the confirmation step before you press Confirm and save payment.

Where is the button to add a payment that has no request behind it?

There is not one. Opening the payment form without naming a request shows a picker of approved requests and a note that free payment entry has been removed. Payments recorded before that change are still in the ledger as history.

A request I need to pay is not in the queue.

It is either not approved yet, on hold, or somebody has already claimed it. The tabs across the top of the queue split exactly those cases — the payment queue explains each one.

I paid the vendor more than was approved.

The form refuses an amount above the approved figure and says so. Paying more is a different obligation: the request is cancelled and a new one is raised for the right amount.
