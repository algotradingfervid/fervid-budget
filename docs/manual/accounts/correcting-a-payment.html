Correcting or voiding a payment

What can still be changed after a payment is recorded, and what is sealed.

Almost nothing. A payment that settled a request is the outcome of that request, and the requester and the approver were both told what it said. Editing it afterwards would rewrite what they were told, so the product does not allow it — not by you, not by an administrator, not at all.

The confirmation step says this before you save: confirming saves the payment, and it cannot be edited or cancelled afterwards. The saved payment repeats it, and its detail card carries a Read-only badge.

A settled payment is final

Saved payments cannot be rewritten. Explain mistakes in the request conversation and follow your accounting correction process. A live installment balance can be paid against the same request; an accepted full settlement or shortfall remains closed. Recoverable returns and referenced reconciliations have a separate recovery recording workflow, which does not edit the original payout.

What can still be changed

One kind of payment is still editable: a payment with no request behind it. These are the historical rows recorded before every payment had to belong to an approved request. They are still ledger entries, so they are still corrected the old way.

Kind of paymentViewEditRemove
Settled against a requestYesNoNo
No request behind it, month open, not already removedYesYesYes
No request behind it, month lockedYesNo — the ledger shows Locked in place of the controls, and the form itself opens read-onlyNo
Already removedYesNoNo — the reason it was removed is shown instead

The Actions column is where you can tell the two kinds apart at a glance. Opening the edit address for a payment that settled a request takes you to the payment itself rather than to a form whose Save could only ever fail.

Removing a payment

Removing — voiding — takes a payment out of the actual totals without deleting it. The row stays in the ledger, marked Removed, and shows the reason it was removed. It is offered on the same rows Edit is: a payment with no request behind it, in a month that is not locked.

  1. In the ledger, select Remove in the payment’s Actions column.
  2. Type a Reason. It is required.

    The browser asks you to confirm removing the payment from actual totals before anything happens.

  3. Select Remove.

The payment’s own screen offers the same thing under Void Payment, with the same required reason. Switch the ledger’s Status filter to Removed / voided to list everything that has been taken out this way.

When a settled payment is wrong

Work out which of these it is, and the route follows.

What is wrongWhat to do
You paid less than approved and marked it fully settledNothing changes the payment. Say so in the conversation on the request; the balance needs a fresh request.
The reference or the note is wrongPost a comment on the request with the correct detail. The trail then carries both, in order.
The approver disputes the shortfallThey raise a concern and the request stays in review — see raising a concern.
The money went to the wrong payeeThat is an accounting adjustment outside this product. Record what happened in the conversation so the trail explains it.
Can an administrator edit a settled payment?

No. The rule sits in the store, under every screen, so it holds for every role.

I attached the wrong bank advice.

A payment tied to a request stops accepting documents the moment it is saved. Post the right one’s details in the conversation on the request.

The Edit form opened but everything is greyed out.

The payment belongs to a locked or voided period, and the banner at the top says so. A locked month is opened again by an administrator — see monthly plans and locking.

Does removing a payment reopen the request?

The question does not arise: only a payment with no request behind it can be removed.
