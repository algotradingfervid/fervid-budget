Manual / Getting started

Signing in

How to get into Fervid Budget, and what to do when it will not let you.

Fervid Budget runs in a web browser. Your organisation will have given you an address to open and an account to sign in with — there is nothing to install.

Signing in

  1. Open the address your organisation gave you.
  2. Type the email address your account was created with.
  3. Type your password.
  4. Select Sign in.

    You land on your home screen, which is tailored to what your account is allowed to do.

When it will not let you in

If the email and password do not match an account, the product says so and lets you try again. It deliberately does not tell you which of the two was wrong, because that would tell anybody typing at your keyboard whether an address has an account behind it.

There is no self-service password reset. If you cannot get in, ask an administrator — they can set a new password for you from the People and accounts screen.

What happens next

What you see after signing in depends entirely on the roles your account holds. Two colleagues signing in side by side can get quite different screens: one may see a queue of approvals, the other only their own requests. Finding your way around explains how to read your own menu.

Shared computers

Sign out when you are finished on a machine other people use. The Log out control sits at the bottom of the sidebar, under your name.

I have forgotten my password.

Ask an administrator to set a new one. They do this from the people screen, and it takes effect immediately.

My colleague can see a screen that I cannot.

That is roles, not a fault. Each screen is tied to a permission, and your menu only lists what your roles carry. See the permission matrix for exactly which role reaches what.
