Manual / Getting started

Signing in

Sign in, recover access, and return to the page you were opening.

Sign in

  1. Open your organisation's Fervid Budget address.
  2. Enter your account email and password.
  3. Select Sign in.

If you came from a protected page, successful sign-in returns you to that permitted destination. Otherwise your role-based home screen opens. Invalid credentials use a general error without identifying which field was wrong.

Can't sign in?

Select Can't sign in? on the login page. If email recovery is configured, request a reset using your account email. The response does not reveal whether an account exists. An eligible account receives a single-use reset link valid for 30 minutes; resetting the password also signs out existing sessions.

If email recovery is unavailable or the message does not arrive, the help page names your administrator/operator contact. Use your organisation's usual support channel when the displayed address is local-only. An administrator can reset or unlock an account from Users.

Access depends on roles

Successful sign-in does not grant access to every screen. Your menu and records depend on your roles and their data scopes. Ask an administrator about missing access.

Shared computers

Use Log out when finished. Do not share reset links or leave a signed-in browser open for other people.
