Every rupee starts as a request someone approved

A mobile-first redesign of the whole application, built around the payment-request workflow agreed over ten rounds of discussion. 43 screens, four personas, one design system extended from the one already running in production.

43 screens4 personasMobile + desktop ClickableExtends fervid-ds.css

How to use these mockups

The bar pinned at the top of every screen is the prototype control, not part of the product.

01 Switch persona

Employee, Manager, Accounts, Administrator. The navigation, the work areas and the action buttons all change. Try the same request detail screen as each of the four.

02 Switch device

Mobile renders a 390 px phone; desktop renders the full sidebar layout. Everything is reachable on both — no screen is desktop-only.

03 Click through

Links follow the real flow. Forms react: change a request type, tab out of an amount, open a settlement confirmation, expand a project on the grid.

Deliberately not built

Named here so a later reader knows these were decided, not forgotten.

Repayment tracking of recoverables · forfeiture or conversion of EMD and PBG · refunds and reversals · editing a recorded payment · tax computation · multi-level approval · bulk approval · copy a previous request · private internal comments · drafts.

The status model, the roles screen and the navigation all leave room for these to arrive later without a redesign.
